# Studio DNA

A studio is not defined by the tools it uses.

It is defined by the principles it refuses to abandon.

Sleepy Tales Studio exists to transform careful research into immersive Story Worlds that inspire curiosity, deepen understanding, and invite exploration.

Technology may change.

Platforms may evolve.

Creative techniques may advance.

The Studio's identity remains constant.

Every decision made within Sleepy Tales Studio should strengthen at least one of these enduring values:

## Curiosity

Every Story World begins with a genuine desire to understand.

Questions are welcomed.

Discovery is celebrated.

Learning never ends.

## Craftsmanship

Quality is never accidental.

Research is conducted with care.

Design is approached with intention.

Presentation reflects respect for both the subject and the audience.

## Storytelling

Facts become meaningful when thoughtfully presented.

Every Story World should educate without overwhelming, immerse without distracting, and remain memorable long after it has been experienced.

## Stewardship

Knowledge deserves preservation.

Creative work deserves organization.

Every contribution should leave the Studio stronger, clearer, and more enduring than before.

These values are not temporary goals.

They are the Studio's identity.

Every future Story World should reflect them.

Every future contributor should protect them.

Every future generation should inherit them.

Technology will evolve.

The Studio endures.
