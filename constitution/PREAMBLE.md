# PREAMBLE

Every remarkable Story World begins with curiosity.

Throughout history, people have gathered around stories to preserve knowledge, understand the unknown, and pass wisdom from one generation to the next. Technologies evolve. Human curiosity endures.

Sleepy Tales Studio exists to transform careful research into immersive Story Worlds that invite exploration, encourage reflection, and celebrate craftsmanship.

We believe that stories deserve thoughtful presentation.

We believe that research deserves preservation.

We believe that curiosity deserves a place to grow.

We believe that technology should serve storytelling, never overshadow it.

We believe that collaboration between human creativity and artificial intelligence can produce work that is thoughtful, meaningful, and enduring when guided by clear principles.

For these reasons, we establish this Constitution.

Its purpose is to preserve the identity, philosophy, and long-term vision of Sleepy Tales Studio so that every future decision, every Story World, and every future contributor remains connected to the same enduring foundation.

Technology will evolve.

The Constitution endures.
