# Story Cards

A Story Card is the smallest complete unit of knowledge within Sleepy Tales Studio.

Each Story Card captures one clearly defined subject drawn from a Research Book.

Rather than storing large amounts of unrelated information together, the Studio organizes knowledge into focused, reusable cards.

Every Story Card should answer a single question, explain a single concept, describe a single event, or document a single person, place, object, or idea.

Each Story Card should remain understandable on its own while also connecting naturally to related Story Cards.

A complete Story Card typically contains:

## Title

A clear and descriptive name.

## Summary

A concise explanation of the subject.

## Verified Information

Research supported by reliable sources.

## Visual References

Illustrations, photographs, maps, diagrams, or other imagery that enrich understanding.

## Narrative Value

An explanation of why this subject matters within the Story World.

## Connections

Links to related Story Cards, timelines, locations, people, or events.

Story Cards are designed for reuse.

A single Story Card may contribute to multiple Story Worlds without requiring the research to be repeated.

Over time, the Studio's Story Card collection becomes a growing knowledge network rather than a collection of isolated documents.

Every Story World strengthens the next.
