# Studio Standards

Every Story World published by Sleepy Tales Studio represents the Studio itself.

For that reason, quality is never an afterthought.

It is designed into every stage of the creative process.

Studio Standards exist to ensure that each Story World reflects the same commitment to research, craftsmanship, clarity, and audience experience, regardless of subject or medium.

Every completed project should be evaluated against five enduring standards.

## Accuracy

Facts should be verified through reliable sources whenever possible.

Uncertainty should be acknowledged rather than hidden.

## Clarity

Complex ideas should become understandable without sacrificing depth or nuance.

## Atmosphere

Every creative decision should strengthen the emotional identity of the Story World.

Visuals, narration, sound, pacing, and design should work together to create a cohesive experience.

## Consistency

The finished work should remain faithful to the intentions established in Genesis, the Project Brief, and the Story World.

## Longevity

Projects should be created with future relevance in mind.

Research, assets, and creative decisions should remain valuable beyond a single publication.

Meeting these standards does not guarantee perfection.

Instead, they provide a shared definition of excellence that guides every contributor toward the Studio's highest aspirations.
