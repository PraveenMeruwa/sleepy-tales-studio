# Production Standards

Production is the disciplined process of transforming a Story World into a finished audience experience.

Its purpose is not merely to assemble creative assets.

Its purpose is to preserve the integrity of the Story World throughout every stage of creation.

Every production undertaken by Sleepy Tales Studio should remain faithful to the research, intentions, and creative direction established during earlier stages of the Creative Storytelling Pipeline.

Production Standards are guided by the following principles.

## Fidelity to the Story World

Creative decisions should strengthen the established Story World rather than contradict it.

Research remains the foundation of production.

## Consistency Across Departments

Writing, visuals, narration, sound, editing, and design should work together as a unified creative effort.

Each discipline contributes to a single audience experience.

## Deliberate Craftsmanship

Every production stage deserves thoughtful attention.

Shortcuts that diminish clarity, atmosphere, or credibility should be avoided.

## Repeatable Excellence

Production workflows should be documented, organized, and capable of producing consistent quality across multiple Story Worlds.

## Continuous Improvement

Each completed production should strengthen the Studio's future work through reflection, refinement, and shared learning.

Production Standards exist to ensure that every published work reflects the Studio's identity regardless of scale, technology, or creative medium.
