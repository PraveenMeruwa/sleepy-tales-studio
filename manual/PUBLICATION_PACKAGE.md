# Publication Package

A Story World is considered ready for publication only when its creative, technical, and archival components have been prepared as a complete and coherent whole.

The Publication Package is the collection of materials required to present a Story World to its intended audience while preserving the Studio's standards of quality, consistency, and long-term stewardship.

A complete Publication Package may include:

* The final published work
* Supporting metadata
* Descriptions and summaries
* Visual assets
* Audio assets
* Credits and acknowledgements
* Licensing information where applicable
* Archival references
* Version identification
* Distribution-specific materials

The exact contents of a Publication Package may differ depending on the medium through which the Story World is shared.

Regardless of format, every Publication Package should satisfy three principles.

## Completeness

The audience should receive a polished and self-contained experience.

## Consistency

Every published element should faithfully represent the Story World and the Studio's identity.

## Preservation

The materials required to understand, reproduce, or revisit the publication should remain accessible through the Story Archive.

Publication marks the beginning of a Story World's relationship with its audience.

It is therefore approached with the same care that guided its creation.
