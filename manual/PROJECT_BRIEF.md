# Project Brief

Genesis establishes that a Story World is worth creating.

The Project Brief explains how that journey should begin.

A Project Brief is the Studio's first working document for any new Story World.

It transforms purpose into direction.

Rather than collecting every available fact, the Project Brief establishes the creative intentions that will guide all later research and production.

Every Project Brief should clearly define:

## The Core Question

What central mystery, event, idea, or experience will the Story World explore?

## The Creative Purpose

Why should this Story World exist?

Why is it meaningful?

## The Intended Audience

Who is this Story World designed for?

What expectations, emotions, or experiences should be considered?

## The Creative Direction

What atmosphere, pacing, visual identity, narrative voice, and emotional tone should define the experience?

## Success Criteria

How will the Studio know that the Story World has achieved its purpose?

The Project Brief is not expected to contain complete research.

Instead, it provides the creative compass that keeps every later decision aligned with the original vision.

Every contributor should understand the Project Brief before beginning significant creative work.

A clear beginning creates a clearer journey.
