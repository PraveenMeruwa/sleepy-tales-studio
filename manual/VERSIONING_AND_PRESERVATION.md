# Versioning & Preservation

Sleepy Tales Studio recognizes that creative knowledge evolves.

Research expands.

Technologies improve.

New evidence emerges.

Creative understanding deepens.

Versioning exists to record that evolution without erasing the Studio's history.

Preservation exists to ensure that previous work remains understandable and accessible.

Every significant revision should be documented in a manner that allows future contributors to understand:

* what changed,
* why it changed,
* when it changed,
* and which version became authoritative.

Historical versions should not be discarded simply because newer versions exist.

They form part of the Studio's creative history and provide valuable context for future decisions.

Whenever practical, updates should preserve backward understanding while improving future practice.

The Studio values thoughtful evolution over constant reinvention.

Its objective is not to remain unchanged.

Its objective is to grow without losing continuity.

Every version becomes part of the Studio's story.
