# Publishing Protocol

Publishing is the formal act through which a Story World becomes part of the permanent body of work created by Sleepy Tales Studio.

Publication represents a commitment to craftsmanship, integrity, and long-term stewardship.

Every publication should complete the following stages before being recognized as an official Studio work.

## Review

The Story World has undergone thoughtful evaluation in accordance with the Review & Approval Workflow.

## Approval

The responsible contributors confirm that the work faithfully represents the Studio's standards at the time of publication.

## Publication

The approved work is released through its intended medium while preserving the supporting materials required for future reference.

## Verification

The published work is confirmed to have been successfully released, archived, and documented.

## Ratification

The Studio formally recognizes the publication as part of its permanent creative record.

Ratification acknowledges that the Story World has entered the Studio's historical archive.

Future revisions remain possible through the Versioning & Preservation process without altering the historical significance of the original publication.

Publishing is therefore not merely the completion of production.

It is the beginning of the Story World's enduring relationship with its audience.
