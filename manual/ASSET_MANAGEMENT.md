# Asset Management

Every creative work produced by Sleepy Tales Studio generates assets.

These assets represent the Studio's accumulated knowledge, craftsmanship, and creative investment.

Asset Management is the discipline of organizing, preserving, and maintaining those resources so they remain understandable, discoverable, and reusable over time.

Assets include, but are not limited to:

* Research documents
* Story Cards
* Images and illustrations
* Video clips
* Audio recordings
* Music and ambience
* Narration
* Maps and diagrams
* Project files
* Production templates
* Published deliverables

Every asset should be managed according to four enduring principles.

## Organization

Assets should be stored within a logical structure that supports efficient discovery and long-term maintenance.

## Traceability

The origin, purpose, and relationship of every significant asset should remain understandable throughout its lifecycle.

## Reusability

Whenever appropriate, assets should be created with future Story Worlds in mind, reducing unnecessary duplication while preserving historical integrity.

## Preservation

Creative work should remain accessible beyond the completion of a single project.

The Studio values its creative history as an enduring resource rather than temporary production material.

Well-managed assets strengthen every future Story World.

Poorly managed assets require knowledge to be rediscovered.

The Studio therefore treats Asset Management as an essential creative discipline rather than an administrative task.
