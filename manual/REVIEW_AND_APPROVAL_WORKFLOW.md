# Review & Approval Workflow

Every Story World deserves thoughtful review before publication.

The purpose of review is not to delay creativity.

Its purpose is to strengthen clarity, consistency, craftsmanship, and confidence.

Review is a collaborative process that confirms a Story World is ready to represent Sleepy Tales Studio.

The Review & Approval Workflow is guided by the following principles.

## Respect the Story World

Review should strengthen the intended creative vision rather than replace it.

Feedback exists to improve the work, not redefine its purpose.

## Verify Before Publishing

Historical facts, creative decisions, technical quality, and production completeness should be evaluated before approval.

Questions should be resolved whenever practical.

Remaining uncertainty should be acknowledged transparently.

## Encourage Constructive Collaboration

Reviews should focus on ideas, evidence, and craftsmanship.

Contributors are encouraged to challenge assumptions respectfully while remaining aligned with the Studio's shared principles.

## Document Significant Decisions

Major revisions, approvals, and exceptions should be recorded whenever they influence the Story World's long-term history.

## Publish With Confidence

Approval signifies that the Story World faithfully represents the Studio's standards at the time of publication.

Future revisions remain possible through the Versioning & Preservation process.

Review concludes when thoughtful evaluation has been completed.

Approval begins the Story World's public life.
