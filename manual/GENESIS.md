# Genesis

Every Story World begins with a decision.

Among countless ideas, questions, and possibilities, one is chosen.

That decision is called Genesis.

Genesis is the formal beginning of a Story World within Sleepy Tales Studio.

It marks the moment when curiosity becomes commitment.

Once a project enters Genesis, it receives the Studio's full creative attention.

Research begins with intention.

Questions become structured investigations.

Ideas begin transforming into experiences.

The purpose of Genesis is not to produce answers immediately.

Its purpose is to establish a strong creative foundation upon which every later stage can confidently build.

Every Genesis should answer four essential questions:

**Why is this Story World worth creating?**

**What makes it meaningful?**

**Who is it intended to serve?**

**What feeling should remain after the journey is complete?**

Only after these questions have been thoughtfully considered should the Story World advance into deeper research and design.

A strong Genesis reduces uncertainty.

It provides direction without limiting creativity.

It reminds every contributor why the journey began.
