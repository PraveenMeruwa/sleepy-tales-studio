# The Studio Workflow

Every Story World follows a journey.

While individual projects may differ in subject, genre, or presentation, they all move through the same creative lifecycle.

The Studio Workflow provides the common structure that guides every project from its earliest idea to its long-term preservation.

The workflow consists of eight stages:

1. **Inspiration**
   A question, mystery, historical event, or idea worthy of exploration.

2. **Research**
   Gathering reliable information, references, timelines, and supporting evidence.

3. **Story World Design**
   Defining the narrative, atmosphere, visual identity, audience experience, and creative direction.

4. **Creative Pipeline**
   Transforming research into structured assets, scripts, visuals, audio, and production-ready materials through the Studio's creative systems.

5. **Production**
   Creating the final experience using the chosen tools, technologies, and workflows.

6. **Publishing**
   Delivering the completed Story World to its intended audience while maintaining consistency with Studio standards.

7. **Preservation**
   Archiving research, assets, decisions, and lessons learned so that every project remains a permanent part of the Studio's knowledge.

8. **Evolution**
   Revisiting completed Story Worlds as new discoveries, technologies, or creative opportunities emerge.

No stage exists in isolation.

Each strengthens the next.

The Studio Workflow ensures that every Story World is created with consistency, intention, and respect for both the creative process and the audience.
