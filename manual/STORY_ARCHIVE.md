# Story Archive

Every completed Story World becomes part of the permanent history of Sleepy Tales Studio.

The Story Archive is the Studio's enduring collection of completed creative works, supporting materials, and historical records.

Its purpose is not merely to preserve finished projects.

Its purpose is to ensure that every Story World remains accessible for future learning, refinement, expansion, and reinterpretation.

Each archived Story World should preserve, whenever practical:

* Genesis
* Project Brief
* Research Book
* Story Cards
* Production Assets
* Published Deliverables
* Version History
* Creative Decisions
* Lessons Learned

Archived Story Worlds should remain discoverable through consistent organization and meaningful documentation.

New discoveries, historical evidence, or technological advances may justify future editions or expansions.

When such evolution occurs, the original work should remain preserved as part of the Studio's historical record.

The Story Archive reflects one of the Studio's central beliefs:

Creative work gains value when it remains available for future generations of creators.
