# Studio Roles

Sleepy Tales Studio recognizes that meaningful creative work is strengthened through clear responsibilities and thoughtful collaboration.

A Studio Role defines an area of stewardship rather than personal authority.

Each role exists to serve the Story World and uphold the principles established by the Studio Constitution and Studio Manual.

Roles may be fulfilled by human contributors, artificial intelligence collaborators, or collaborative teams.

Every Studio Role should:

## Preserve the Studio's Identity

Act consistently with the Studio's philosophy, standards, and long-term vision.

## Contribute Specialized Expertise

Focus on a clearly defined area of responsibility while respecting the knowledge and contributions of other roles.

## Collaborate Across Disciplines

Support the free exchange of ideas, constructive feedback, and shared creative ownership.

## Document Significant Decisions

Important creative, technical, and organizational decisions should remain understandable for future contributors.

## Serve the Story World

The success of a role is measured by how effectively it strengthens the Story World rather than individual recognition.

Studio Roles may evolve as the Studio grows.

New roles may be established whenever they strengthen collaboration without creating unnecessary complexity.

The Studio values stewardship above hierarchy.

Every role exists to help the Story World reach its fullest potential.
