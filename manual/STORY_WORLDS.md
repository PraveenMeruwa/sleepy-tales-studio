# Story Worlds

A Story World is the complete body of knowledge, atmosphere, relationships, and creative interpretation surrounding a single subject.

It is the highest-level creative artifact produced by Sleepy Tales Studio.

A Story World is not defined by a single video, article, podcast, or presentation.

Instead, it exists independently of any one medium.

Every Story World is built from many interconnected elements, including:

* Genesis
* Project Brief
* Research Book
* Story Cards
* Timelines
* People
* Places
* Events
* Evidence
* Visual References
* Audio Direction
* Narrative Structure
* Production Assets

Together, these elements form a coherent creative universe.

From a single Story World, the Studio may produce multiple creative experiences without repeating research or rediscovering knowledge.

Every new project should strengthen the Story World rather than replace it.

As new evidence emerges, Story Worlds may evolve while preserving their historical record and creative integrity.

The Studio therefore treats Story Worlds as living creative ecosystems rather than finished products.

The audience may experience only one expression of a Story World.

The Studio preserves the entire world.
