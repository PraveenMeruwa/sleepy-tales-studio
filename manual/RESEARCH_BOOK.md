# Research Book

Every remarkable Story World is built upon trusted knowledge.

The Research Book is the Studio's central repository of verified understanding for a single Story World.

Its purpose is not to collect every available source.

Its purpose is to organize knowledge into a form that can guide thoughtful storytelling.

A Research Book should become the definitive reference for everyone contributing to the project.

It should remain clear, structured, traceable, and continually refined as new discoveries emerge.

A complete Research Book typically includes:

## Historical Context

The broader background necessary to understand the Story World.

## Timeline

A chronological record of significant events.

## People

The individuals whose actions, decisions, or experiences shaped the Story World.

## Places

Locations that define the narrative and provide geographical context.

## Evidence

Documents, photographs, maps, interviews, reports, and other verified sources that support the research.

## Open Questions

Unresolved mysteries, competing theories, conflicting accounts, and areas requiring further investigation.

## Creative Opportunities

Ideas, visual moments, emotional themes, and narrative possibilities discovered during research.

The Research Book is a living knowledge base.

It grows through careful verification rather than speculation.

Every later stage of the Creative Storytelling Pipeline should rely upon it as the Studio's primary source of understanding.
