# First Glimpse

Every Story World has a moment of arrival.

Before the audience understands the narrative...

before they hear the full story...

before they discover the mystery...

they experience a First Glimpse.

The First Glimpse is the Studio's philosophy for designing unforgettable beginnings.

Its purpose is not to explain.

Its purpose is to awaken curiosity.

A successful First Glimpse should invite the audience to ask questions rather than immediately provide answers.

It establishes atmosphere before information.

Emotion before explanation.

Wonder before certainty.

Whether experienced through an opening scene, an illustration, a cinematic sequence, a thumbnail, a title, or a single sentence, the First Glimpse should leave the audience wanting to step further into the Story World.

Every Story World deserves a beginning that feels intentional.

A beginning that quietly promises:

"There is something worth discovering here."

The audience may forget individual facts.

They rarely forget how the journey began.

For this reason, the First Glimpse should always be designed with the same care and craftsmanship as the Story World itself.
