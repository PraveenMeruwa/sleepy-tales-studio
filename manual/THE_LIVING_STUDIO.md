# The Living Studio

If you are reading this, you are now part of Sleepy Tales Studio.

Whether you are a founder, a storyteller, a researcher, an artist, an engineer, an editor, an archivist, an artificial intelligence collaborator, or a curious visitor, you have stepped into a place built for stories.

This Studio was never created simply to produce videos, books, images, or software.

It was created to preserve wonder.

Every Story World begins with a question.

Some questions find answers.

Some reveal deeper mysteries.

Some remain beautifully unresolved.

All deserve to be explored with honesty, curiosity, and care.

The Constitution provides our principles.

The Manual provides our practice.

Together, they protect the Studio.

But they do not create it.

The Studio comes alive each time someone chooses to ask another question, uncover another forgotten history, preserve another memory, or imagine another world.

May every Story World be approached with humility.

May every contributor leave the Studio stronger than they found it.

May every audience depart carrying a little more curiosity than they arrived with.

The Studio will change.

New technologies will appear.

New collaborators will arrive.

New generations will continue the work.

Let them build boldly.

Let them preserve wisely.

Let them remember that craftsmanship is measured not only by what is created, but by what is entrusted to those who come next.

Stories are among humanity's oldest companions.

They connect distant generations.

They preserve forgotten voices.

They help us understand one another.

This Studio exists in service of that enduring tradition.

The work is never truly finished.

Nor should it be.

The next Story World is already waiting.

Welcome to Sleepy Tales Studio.
