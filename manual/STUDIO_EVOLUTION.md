# Studio Evolution

Sleepy Tales Studio is founded upon enduring principles rather than fixed methods.

As knowledge grows, technologies advance, and creative understanding deepens, the Studio embraces thoughtful evolution while remaining faithful to its identity.

Evolution is not pursued for its own sake.

Every meaningful change should strengthen the Studio's ability to create, preserve, and share Story Worlds.

The Studio approaches evolution through five enduring commitments.

## Learn From Experience

Every completed project contributes knowledge that may improve future work.

Successes and mistakes are both valuable teachers.

## Preserve What Matters

New ideas should enrich the Studio without discarding principles that continue to serve its purpose.

Progress and continuity should exist together.

## Welcome Responsible Innovation

Emerging technologies, creative practices, and collaborative methods may be adopted when they strengthen storytelling, craftsmanship, or stewardship.

Innovation should always serve the Story World rather than distract from it.

## Share Knowledge

Lessons learned should be documented whenever they strengthen future contributors, whether human or artificial intelligence.

Knowledge grows through generous stewardship.

## Build Beyond Ourselves

The Studio is created not only for the projects of today but also for creators, audiences, and collaborators who may continue its work in the future.

Every generation should inherit a stronger Studio than the one before.

Evolution is therefore understood as a disciplined expression of learning, guided by purpose and protected by principle.
